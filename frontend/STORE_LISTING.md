## Full Description (4000 chars max)
No Limit Delivery brings everything you need right to your doorstep in Emalahleni, Mpumalanga.

Order from your favourite local restaurants, schedule a laundry pickup, send a parcel across town, surprise someone with fresh flowers, or get pharmacy essentials delivered — all from one app.

FOOD DELIVERY
Browse 9+ local restaurants including Pedro's Chicken, Mochachos, Shawarma Express, CAFE E, #Braai, Jazbar Indian Cuisine, and more. From flame-grilled chicken to authentic Durban curries, artisan coffee to braai boxes — your cravings are covered.

LAUNDRY SERVICES
Chantelly's Laundry Services picks up, cleans, and delivers your clothes, blankets, curtains, and carpets. Professional dry cleaning, wash & fold, and ironing services available.

PARCEL DELIVERY
Send parcels across Emalahleni with same-day and express delivery options. Standard and express tiers with packaging available.

FRESH FLOWERS
Beautiful bouquets, roses, and arrangements delivered for any occasion. Add chocolates, greeting cards, or a teddy bear.

PHARMACY
Health supplements, skincare, weight management, and clinic services from Witmed Pharmacy & Clinic.

KEY FEATURES
- Browse 13+ local service providers
- Real-time order tracking
- Multiple payment options: Card, SnapScan, instant EFT, Cash on Delivery
- Secure payments powered by PayFast
- Special instructions & allergy alerts
- Tip your driver
- Promo codes & loyalty rewards
- Map-based address selection

PAYMENT OPTIONS
Pay securely online via PayFast (Visa, Mastercard, SnapScan, instant EFT), cash on delivery, or manual bank transfer.

Proudly serving Emalahleni, Mpumalanga, South Africa.
